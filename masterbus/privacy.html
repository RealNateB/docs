MasterBus – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Overview

MasterBus is a private message routing service operated by Nathan Brown at masterbus.up.railway.app. It connects the operator's own apps, devices, and assistants. Access is by invitation only: sign-in succeeds only for email addresses the operator has registered in advance. This policy explains what data MasterBus handles and how.

Google Sign-In

When you sign in with Google, MasterBus requests the openid, email, and profile scopes. It uses your email address to check that you are a registered user, and stores your email address, your name, and the time of your last sign-in with your account record. It does not store your Google password or your Google access token.

Gmail Integration

The operator may connect a Gmail account to MasterBus. For a connected account, MasterBus stores an OAuth refresh token and uses it to receive notifications of new mail, read those messages, and send mail when the account owner asks it to. Message content is delivered only to the account owner's own apps and assistants on the bus. Those assistants may use AI services the owner has configured, solely to provide features to that owner.

Limited Use

MasterBus's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not sold, not used for advertising, not read by humans except with the account owner's consent or where required for security or by law, and not used to develop, improve, or train generalized AI or machine learning models.

Other Data

MasterBus keeps the records it needs to run securely: sign-in sessions (refresh tokens are stored only as hashes), API key hashes, an audit log of security events with the network address they came from, and the messages passing through the bus. Server logs are kept by the hosting provider.

Storage and Sharing

Data is stored on servers hosted by Railway in the United States. MasterBus does not share your data with third parties except the hosting provider, the services you or the operator connect (such as Google), and where required by law.

Retention and Deletion

Account records are kept until the operator removes your access. To have your data deleted, or to disconnect a Gmail account, contact the address below. You can also revoke MasterBus's access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
